## Brand & Style

This design system embodies mindful nutrition, quiet encouragement, and holistic clarity. Drawing from organic modernism, Scandinavian wellness editorial design, and the humane restraint of Apple Health, it transforms food logging from an anxious tallying of calories into an intentional, grounding daily ritual.

The visual tone is airy, nourishing, and warm. Rather than harsh alerts or dark clinical dashboards, interactions are smooth, soft-spoken, and organic. Tactile, pill-shaped markers and deeply rounded cards sit upon sunlit, herbal off-white surfaces, offering users a space of restorative calm.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical tones, warm sun-dried grains, and mineral neutrals:
- **Primary (`#15803D`)**: Deep matcha / leaf green representing vitality, active balance, and intentional progress. Used for key interaction anchors, selected states, and protein progress indicators.
- **Secondary (`#65A30D`)**: Avocado olive green representing healthy fats, freshness, and secondary supportive highlights. Paired with a delicate light wash (`#ECFCCB`) for chips and data badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`)**: Warm golden grain representing complex carbohydrates, energy balance, and daytime meal notifications. Paired with a soft amber base (`#FEF3C7`).
- **Neutral (`#1F2937`)**: Earthy warm slate delivering legible, low-fatigue typography, shifting away from harsh pure blacks.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base**: `#F8FAF7` (subtle botanical parchment).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards)**: `#FCFDF9` to `#FFFFFF` with whisper borders (`#E5E7EB` / `#DCFCE7`).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Nested / Insets)**: `#ECFDF5` (dewy mint) and `#F0FDF4`.
